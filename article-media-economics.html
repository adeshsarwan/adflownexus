MEDIA BUYING · INSIGHT

The new economics of cross-channel media buying.

Buying more impressions is easy. Building a media system that knows what each channel is supposed to accomplish — and what it is worth — is harder.
ADFLOW NEXUS · 6 MIN READ
Channel rolesPrice vs valueMeasurementConnected system

Cross-channel media works when channels are assigned jobs within the customer journey instead of being forced to win the same attribution contest.

Give every channel a job.

Paid search is excellent at harvesting declared intent. Social and video can create or shape demand. Native can place a proposition inside a discovery environment. Programmatic can add reach, frequency control and re-engagement. Direct publisher relationships can create context and access that auctions do not always provide.

The plan becomes clearer when we define the role first and the inventory second.

Cheap media is not necessarily efficient media.

CPM and CPC describe the price of access, not the business value created. A more expensive impression delivered in a relevant context to a receptive audience can outperform a large volume of low-cost exposure.

Reach quality

Are we reaching people capable of becoming customers?

Attention

Does the environment give the message a chance to work?

Outcome

Does exposure contribute to valuable business action?

MEDIA PRINCIPLE

Optimise the cost of creating a valuable outcome — not the cost of buying an isolated media unit.

Use attribution as evidence, not absolute truth.

Different platforms observe different parts of the journey and naturally tend to credit themselves. We use platform reporting for operational decisions, but pair it with site analytics, conversion quality, blended business metrics and experiments where practical.

Connect media to creative and conversion.

Media performance cannot be separated from what is shown after the impression or click. A channel can appear inefficient because the creative is mismatched, the landing experience is weak or the offer is unclear.

Our Nexus model joins media buying with creative testing, content, landing-page optimisation and business measurement so each layer can improve the others.
