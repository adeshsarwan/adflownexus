PERFORMANCE MARKETING · INSIGHT

From campaign optimisation to capital allocation.

The most useful question is not “which campaign has the best metric?” It is “where should the next dollar go to create the most valuable incremental growth?”
ADFLOW NEXUS · 7 MIN READ
Start with economicsIncremental growthPortfolio viewOperating loop

Marketing becomes more powerful when media budgets are treated as investment capital rather than fixed campaign allowances.

Start with the business equation.

A low CPA can be excellent or irrelevant depending on customer quality, margin, retention and scale. Before optimising a channel, define what a valuable customer or action means to the business.

Value

Revenue, margin or qualified lead value created.

Cost

Media, creative and conversion costs required.

Capacity

How much spend can be added before returns deteriorate?

Average efficiency is not incremental efficiency.

A campaign reporting a strong historical ROAS does not guarantee the next unit of spend will perform at the same level. As audiences saturate and auctions change, marginal returns can decline.

That is why scaling requires controlled budget movement and observation rather than simply increasing spend on yesterday's winner.

THE CAPITAL ALLOCATION MINDSET

Budget is fluid. Move it toward the combinations of audience, creative, channel and conversion path producing the strongest incremental business return.

Manage a portfolio, not a collection of silos.

Search may capture existing intent while social creates demand. Programmatic can extend reach or re-engage. Content can improve conversion in every channel. Looking only at platform-level attribution can encourage channels to compete for credit instead of working together.

We therefore combine platform signals with business outcomes, funnel behavior and controlled experimentation to form a more useful allocation view.

Build a disciplined allocation loop.

1. Establish guardrails

Define target economics, minimum data requirements and the boundaries within which teams can experiment.

2. Create competing opportunities

Maintain multiple audiences, creative propositions, channels and landing experiences so capital has somewhere productive to move.

3. Reallocate deliberately

Increase investment where evidence is strengthening, reduce it where marginal performance is deteriorating, and preserve enough exploration budget to find the next source of growth.
